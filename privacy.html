Privacy and data

Saved on your device

Traymath stores your food log, weight entries, targets, goals, check-ins, and preferences on your device. No account is required in this version. Traymath does not upload these records or your calculator inputs to a cloud database. Calculator inputs such as age and height are used on your device to estimate targets.

Menu requests

The app retrieves public campus dining menus. Menu providers receive network request information, such as your IP address. Your food logs and measurements are not included in menu requests. Traymath does not include advertising or analytics tracking.

Export and deletion

Settings lets you export your saved data. Clear saved data removes Traymath history and settings from this device. Exported files remain wherever you saved or shared them. Deleting the app may erase its local history. Traymath does not provide automatic cloud backup or syncing in this version; device backups depend on your device settings.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email traymath.support.app@gmail.com.

Traymath is an independent student project and is not an official university service.
