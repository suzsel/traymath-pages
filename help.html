Help and contact

For help, bug reports, or privacy questions, email traymath.support.app@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to a problem. Please leave personal health information out of bug reports unless it is needed to explain the issue.

Common questions

Where do the menus come from?

Traymath reads the public UC Davis dining menus. Published dishes, nutrition, and scheduled hours can change. Each menu dish links to its official source.

Why does a nutrient show a question mark?

The published menu is missing that value. You can enter an estimate before logging a dish or edit a saved entry. Estimates are marked in your log.

How do I keep a copy of my log?

Go to Settings → Preferences → Export my data, then save the JSON file to Files or another location. This version has no account, automatic cloud backup, or in-app import of an exported file.

Can I use Traymath offline?

Your saved history, manual entries, targets, and weight tracking work offline. Updating dining menus requires internet access.

Are nutrition estimates or dietary filters medical advice?

No. Nutrition values and portions may vary. Targets are editable starting estimates for adults, and dietary filters do not verify allergy safety.
